# Turnaround YouTube Demo Package

This package is designed for a 4-6 minute product demonstration. It covers the minimum YouTube upload fields and gives the presenter a word-for-word structure.

## Recommended Video

**Title**

Turnaround: Fleet Operations Intelligence for East African Logistics

**Shorter title option**

Turnaround Demo | Live Fleet Tracking, Dwell Intelligence, and Cost Visibility

**Thumbnail text**

SEE EVERY DELAY

**Thumbnail visual**

Use a clean screenshot of the Corridor Tracker map with one visible delayed vehicle or alert. Add the Turnaround logo and keep the text to 3-4 words. Do not put small paragraphs or a full screenshot full of unreadable UI on the thumbnail.

**Audience**

Fleet managers, dispatchers, logistics operators, and carriers working across East African freight corridors.

**Target length**

4-6 minutes. Keep the screen recording at 1080p and 30 or 60 fps.

## Ready-to-Read Voiceover

### 0:00-0:20 - Opening

"Turnaround is an operational intelligence platform for commercial fleet and corridor logistics. It helps dispatchers see where vehicles are, understand why time is being lost, and connect operational delays to financial impact. In this demo, I will show a typical workflow from the dashboard to live tracking, dispatch, dwell intelligence, and analytics."

### 0:20-0:55 - Dashboard

Open **Dashboard**.

"The dashboard gives the operations team one shared view of the fleet. It brings together active trucks, delayed vehicles, excess dwell, alerts, and financial exposure. Instead of checking separate spreadsheets or interpreting raw GPS points, the team can immediately see which events need attention."

Pause briefly on the most important KPI cards. Point out a delayed vehicle, dwell exposure, or financial impact value if available in the demo data.

### 0:55-1:40 - Corridor Tracker

Open **Corridor Tracker**.

"The Corridor Tracker shows the live operating picture on the route. Vehicles are displayed with their current status, speed, heading, and facility context. The map connects vehicle movement to real operating locations such as ports, depots, warehouses, weighbridges, and border crossings."

Click one vehicle and expand its details.

"Opening a vehicle gives the dispatcher the information needed for a quick decision: the vehicle identity, driver or co-driver, availability, GPS state, current location, and any active dwell. This makes it possible to move from a map signal to an operational action."

### 1:40-2:25 - Dwell and delay intelligence

Open **Delay Alerts** or a vehicle/location detail view.

"Turnaround does more than report that a vehicle stopped. It compares actual time at a facility with the expected dwell baseline, identifies excess minutes, classifies the likely bottleneck, and highlights the resulting risk. This turns an invisible delay into a visible event that a team can investigate."

Show one alert or dwell record.

"For example, a prolonged stop can be associated with excessive dwell, a gate hold, weighbridge congestion, a recurring bottleneck, or an SLA breach. The team can then prioritize the issue based on severity and operational impact."

### 2:25-3:15 - Assets and dispatch

Open **Carrier Assets**, then **Trips**.

"Carrier Assets keeps powered vehicles and cargo equipment organized in the same operating system while preserving the difference between them. Trucks, tankers, trailers, chassis, ships, and containers can be managed according to their operational role. Containers are registered separately and are not treated as driver-assigned vehicles."

Open or create a trip using prepared demo data.

"A trip can connect the vehicle, route, schedule, cargo, customs seal, and available container. This gives dispatchers a consistent way to prepare a movement and keeps the relationships between assets, drivers, facilities, and trips clear."

### 3:15-4:00 - Analytics and financial impact

Open **Analytics**.

"Analytics helps the team move from individual events to recurring patterns. Managers can compare dwell performance across locations, identify bottlenecks, review delay trends, and understand where operational time is being lost."

Point to the cost or demurrage view if populated.

"The financial view translates excess dwell into an estimated operating cost and makes demurrage or SLA exposure easier to prioritize. The goal is not only to know that a truck is delayed, but to understand what the delay means for the business and what action should happen next."

### 4:00-4:30 - AI and closing

Open **Fleet AI** if the demo data supports it.

"Fleet AI provides another way to ask questions about the operating data, such as comparing port clearance times with border crossing dwell or finding the most important delay pattern."

"Turnaround brings fleet tracking, dispatch, geofencing, dwell intelligence, analytics, and financial visibility into one workspace. It is built to help logistics teams make faster decisions across East African corridors. The links to the live demo and source code are in the description."

## YouTube Upload Settings

**Required or normally needed**

- Title
- Video file
- Visibility: choose **Unlisted** for review or **Public** to publish
- Audience setting: select whether the video is made for kids. This product demo should normally be marked **No, it is not made for kids**.

**Recommended**

- Description from the template above
- Custom thumbnail in 1280x720, 16:9, under YouTube's current file-size limit
- Category: **Science & Technology** or **Howto & Style**
- Language: English
- Chapters using the timestamps above
- End screen linking to the live demo or another project video
- Cards only if they add useful context
- Subtitles or an uploaded transcript
- License: the default YouTube license unless a different license is intended
- Comments: enable moderation appropriate for the project

YouTube's exact upload screens and limits can change, so review the current YouTube Studio prompts at upload time. The title, description, video, visibility, and audience declaration are the important fields for this demo; tags and cards are optional.

## Export Recommendation

- Container: MP4
- Video codec: H.264
- Audio codec: AAC
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame rate: 30 fps or the same frame rate used during recording
- Audio: clear voice at a consistent level, with music low enough that every word is understandable
